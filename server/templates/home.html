NES Open Tournament Golf Randomizer

Eighteen holes drawn from NES Open and Mario Open Golf, on one ROM everyone plays.

A seed is a randomized course: its holes, par layout, course music and wind. Everyone who downloads a seed plays the same holes with the same wind, so rounds can be compared shot for shot.

  1. Set up your ROMs. Your ROM files stay in your browser. The site only checks that they are unmodified.
  2. Generate a seed and share its page with the other players.
  3. Download the seed from its page and play it in Stroke Play.
